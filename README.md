# Droste

Droste turns your Mac into frames: full-screen spaces of one or more apps that you step into and out of.

## Get it

**[Download the latest Droste](https://github.com/joeycentofanti/droste-releases/releases/latest)**: on that page, under **Assets**, click the **Droste-….dmg** file. (The "Source code" files there are the code, not the app.)

1. Open the DMG and drag Droste into Applications.
2. Open Droste from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check it: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Droste walks you through the rest (two permissions, then your first frame).

Droste updates itself from here: `appcast.xml` is its update feed.
